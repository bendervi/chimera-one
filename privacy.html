Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

In short: Chimera does not sell your personal data, and the developer never receives it. There are no accounts and no tracking. The free version on iPhone and iPad shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to deliver them; Chimera Pro has no ads. On iPhone and iPad, Google's Cast software also collects anonymous diagnostics so that Chromecast works. Details below.

Using Chimera on Apple TV? See the Apple TV Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

The developer of Chimera runs no servers that receive information from the app, and the app contains no tracking software. The developer collects no data.

Advertising (free version, iPhone and iPad). Without Chimera Pro, the iPhone and iPad app occasionally shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob after you close a video. Ads are never shown to Chimera Pro subscribers or owners, and never in the Mac or Apple TV apps. Chimera requests only non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not available to Google. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's software collects a device identifier, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before any ad is requested; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › General › Ad Privacy Settings. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services).

Google Cast (iPhone and iPad). Chromecast support on iPhone and iPad uses Google's Cast SDK. As stated in its privacy manifest, the SDK collects a device identifier, information about how Cast features are used, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to make casting work and to improve it (analytics). It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. The Mac and Apple TV apps don't include Google's Cast SDK.

Purchases

Chimera Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple, on your device, which Chimera Pro purchase is active. If you start a free trial, Chimera can remind you before it ends with a notification scheduled on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Source credentials (server addresses, usernames, passwords and access tokens) are stored only in your device's Keychain.
  • Your library data — favorites, watch history, playback positions, playlists, downloads and settings — is stored on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Chimera can sync your favorites, history, settings and list of sources (names and server addresses) between your devices through Apple's iCloud. This data is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot access it. Passwords and access tokens are never synced; they stay in each device's Keychain.

Services the app connects to

Chimera connects directly from your device to the services you choose. Those services receive what they need to work (for example your IP address and the credentials you entered) and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Your media sources — the IPTV provider, Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, Navidrome or Subsonic music server, Immich photo server, OPDS book catalog, or Dropbox or MEGA account you add. Plex and Dropbox sign-in happens with those companies directly.
  • Live TV › Sports loads public match schedules from ESPN's scoreboard service. No personal information is sent.
  • Home › Trending Now loads the most-read Wikipedia articles from Wikimedia and looks up which are films or series on Wikidata. Only your device's country and language are sent; nothing about what you watch leaves your device.
  • Books › Project Gutenberg, if you turn it on, loads the free book catalog from gutenberg.org.
  • Missing movie and series details are looked up on Wikidata and Wikipedia using only the title, year and your language (Settings › General › Fill In Missing Details turns this off).
  • Song lyrics are looked up from LRCLIB using a song's title, artist and album.
  • Chromecast uses Google Cast to send the media you choose to a Cast device on your network. The Cast device loads Chimera's receiver page from GitHub Pages, which receives the Cast device's IP address like any website; nothing about you or what you watch is sent to it.
  • AirPlay sends the media you choose to an AirPlay device through Apple's AirPlay.

Local network

Local network access is used to reach media servers on your home network, to find Chromecast devices and stream to them and to AirPlay devices, and to set up Chimera on Apple TV. Nothing found on your network is sent anywhere else.

Children

Chimera does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
