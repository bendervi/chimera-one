Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

In short: Chimera One does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

Data we collect

None. The developer of Chimera One runs no servers that receive information from the app, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Data stored on your device

  • Source credentials (server addresses, usernames, passwords and access tokens) are stored only in your device's Keychain.
  • Your library data — favorites, watch history, playback positions, playlists, downloads and settings — is stored on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Chimera One can sync your favorites, history, settings and list of sources (names and server addresses) between your devices through Apple's iCloud. This data is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot access it. Passwords and access tokens are never synced; they stay in each device's Keychain.

Services the app connects to

Chimera One connects directly from your device to the services you choose. Those services receive what they need to work (for example your IP address and the credentials you entered) and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Your media sources — the IPTV provider, Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, Dropbox or MEGA account you add. Plex and Dropbox sign-in happens with those companies directly.
  • Live TV › Sports loads public match schedules from ESPN's scoreboard service. No personal information is sent.
  • Song lyrics are looked up from LRCLIB using a song's title, artist and album.
  • Chromecast uses Google Cast to send the media you choose to a Cast device on your network.

Local network

Local network access is used only to find Chromecast devices and to set up Chimera One on Apple TV.

Children

Chimera One does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
