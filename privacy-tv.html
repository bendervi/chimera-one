Privacy Policy for Apple TV

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Chimera for Apple TV does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data.

This page covers the Apple TV app. For iPhone, iPad and Mac, see the Chimera Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

None. The developer of Chimera runs no servers that receive information from the app, and the Apple TV app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. Unlike the iPhone and iPad app, it doesn't include Google's Cast SDK.

Data stored on your Apple TV

  • Source credentials (server addresses, usernames, passwords and access tokens) are stored only in your Apple TV's Keychain.
  • Your library data — favorites, watch history, playback positions and settings — is stored on your Apple TV.
  • Top Shelf. To show Continue Watching and Favorite Channels on the Apple TV Home Screen, the app saves a short list of those items (titles, artwork links and playback positions) where its Top Shelf extension can read it. This list never leaves your Apple TV, and passwords and stream links are never included.

iCloud

If your Apple TV is signed in to iCloud, Chimera can sync your favorites, history, settings and list of sources (names and server addresses) with your other devices through Apple's iCloud. This data is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot access it. Passwords and access tokens are never synced; they stay in each device's Keychain.

Adding a source from your iPhone

You can send a source from Chimera on iPhone to your Apple TV by scanning the code shown on the TV. The source travels directly between the two devices over your local network, encrypted with a one-time key contained in that code. It never passes through any server, and the key is discarded when the transfer ends.

Services the app connects to

Chimera connects directly from your Apple TV to the services you choose. Those services receive what they need to work (for example your IP address and the credentials you entered) and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Your media sources — the IPTV provider, Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, Navidrome or Subsonic music server, Immich photo server, or Dropbox or MEGA account you add. Plex and Dropbox sign-in happens with those companies directly.
  • Live TV › Sports loads public match schedules from ESPN's scoreboard service. No personal information is sent.
  • Trending titles on Home are found by loading the most-read Wikipedia articles from Wikimedia and looking up which are films or series on Wikidata. Only your Apple TV's country and language are sent; nothing about what you watch leaves your Apple TV.
  • Missing movie and series details are looked up on Wikidata and Wikipedia using only the title, year and your language (Settings › General › Fill In Missing Details turns this off).

Local network

Local network access is used to reach media servers on your home network and to receive a source sent from your iPhone. Nothing found on your network is sent anywhere else.

Children

Chimera does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
