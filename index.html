Chimera One

A beautiful player for the media you already have — on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Connect your IPTV service (Xtream Codes or M3U playlists), your Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, Dropbox or MEGA, and enjoy Live TV with a full guide, movies, series, music, books and photos in one app designed for Apple devices.

Chimera One doesn't include any content. It plays media from sources you own or are authorized to use.
