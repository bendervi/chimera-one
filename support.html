Frequently asked questions

Does Chimera come with channels or movies?

No. Chimera is a player only. You add your own sources: an IPTV subscription you are authorized to use, your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, or your Dropbox or MEGA files.

How do I add a source?

On first launch, tap Add Your First Source. Later, go to Settings → Manage Sources → Add Source, choose the type, and enter the details from your provider or server.

A video won't play or has no picture.

Chimera automatically switches to its built-in fallback engine for formats Apple's player can't decode. You can also switch engines manually from the player's ⋯ menu. If it still fails, check that the stream plays elsewhere, then contact us.

Can I use AirPlay for video?

Video AirPlay isn't available, because the fallback engine can't send video over AirPlay. Chromecast is supported on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and AirPlay works for music.

What is Chimera Pro?

Chimera is free with up to two sources. Chimera Pro adds unlimited sources, Multiview, Live TV recording, one library across all your sources, your own Home layout and more, and removes ads. It's available as a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. See the plans in Settings → Chimera Pro, or tap any feature marked with a lock.

How do I restore Chimera Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings → Chimera Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Chimera Pro works on every device signed in to that Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Chimera tap Manage Subscription in Settings → Chimera Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why do I see ads?

The free version on iPhone and iPad occasionally shows an ad after you close a video — never during playback, and never on Mac or Apple TV. Ads are non-personalized and Chimera doesn't track you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in Settings → General → Ad Privacy Settings. Chimera Pro removes ads.

Where are my passwords stored?

Only on your device, in the system Keychain. See the Privacy Policy.
